# Privacy and Data Governance — Okoa Sem

Status: Proposed (legal review required)
Jurisdiction: Kenya (initial). Evaluate Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and ODPC guidance.

## 1. Legal baseline (verified vs to review)

Verified (general): Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 establishes principles including lawful processing, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity/confidentiality, accountability. Cross-border transfer restrictions and data subject rights exist.

Requires legal review: exact consent mechanisms for students/minors, institutional data sharing agreements, retention periods, ODPC registration, DPIA requirements, cross-border transfer safeguards for AI providers.

## 2. Consent

- Explicit consent for account creation and data processing.
- Separate consent for AI processing of uploaded content.
- Consent records stored and revocable.

## 3. Purpose limitation

- Data used only for academic coordination.
- No advertising or unrelated profiling.
- AI providers used solely for extraction/assistant.

## 4. Data minimisation

- Collect only necessary profile and academic data.
- Redact unnecessary PII from AI prompts where possible.
- Limit source retention to needed duration.

## 5. Retention

- Proposed: source documents 12 months after term end; audit logs 7 years; notifications 90 days.
- Subject to institutional policy and legal review.

## 6. Deletion

- User deletion request removes PII; academic records may be anonymised.
- Hard purge pipeline with verification.

## 7. Student privacy

- Students see only their own submissions/grades.
- Lecturers see only their courses.
- No public profiles.

## 8. Imported messages

- WhatsApp exports may contain third-party personal data.
- Treat as user-provided; warn users not to share sensitive personal data of others.
- Allow deletion of imported sources.

## 9. AI providers

- Cross-border processing: data sent to AI provider APIs (possibly outside Kenya).
- Require contractual safeguards (DPA) and transparency.
- Allow institution-level opt-out of AI for sensitive data (future).

## 10. Institutional data ownership

- Institution owns institutional academic data.
- Platform is processor; data processing agreement required.
- Export capability for institutions.
