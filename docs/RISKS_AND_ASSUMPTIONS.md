# Risks and Assumptions — Okoa Sem

Status: Proposed

## Product risks

- Low adoption if friction high.
- Users distrust AI; mitigated by verification + provenance.

## Technical risks

- Extraction accuracy insufficient; mitigated by evals and human verification.
- AI cost overruns; mitigated by model selection and rate limits.
- Deployment platform constraints; fallback documented.

## Privacy risks

- Imported messages contain third-party PII.
- Cross-border AI processing; legal review needed.

## Integration risks

- RumptyCloud capabilities unknown.
- Email/WhatsApp integrations deferred.

## Adoption risks

- Lecturers may not verify promptly.
- Class reps may not use consistently.

## Operational risks

- Queue failures cause delays; retries/monitoring.

## Assumptions

- Team has 5-6 members.
- Hackathon timeline.
- Internet access for AI APIs.
- Users can paste/export WhatsApp chats.
